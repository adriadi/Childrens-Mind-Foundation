Typography

Baloo 2 is the display face for headings and buttons; Inter carries body copy. Together they read as friendly but clear.

Corner radii

Rounded corners soften the brand. Small for inputs, medium for stat tiles, large for cards and banners. Buttons use a full pill (99px).

--r-sm · 10px
--r-md · 18px
--r-lg · 28px

Elevation

Two soft, cool-toned shadows. Cards rest on --shadow-card; hover and floating elements lift to --shadow-soft.

--shadow-card
--shadow-soft

Buttons

Pill-shaped, Baloo 2, with a gentle lift-and-shadow on hover. On desktop every button shares a 180px minimum width for tidy rows. Primary (sunset) drives donations; secondary and outline support.
